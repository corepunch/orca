# Fishers' Weather — research

Anglers already pay for big map-and-social apps (Fishbrain, FishAngler, Fishing Points), and they mostly complain about paywalls on features that used to be free, about bite scores they don't trust, and about tides from a station miles away.
Nobody owns the calm, honest "should I go, when, and where on the water" app that ties a private catch log to the conditions and works for lake and coast anglers worldwide.
Fishers' Weather can win by being fast, private and honest about its evidence. It needs tides, water temperature, local units, the user's own places and a log that teaches people their own patterns. It should not try to become another social network.

Research date: November 2026. Prices and ratings change often and differ by storefront. Figures marked *(aggregator)* come from third-party trackers, not the App Store itself, so treat them as approximate. Reddit pages could not be fetched from this environment, and web search returned no Reddit threads. The complaint themes therefore come from App Store reviews, review analyses, forums and vendor pages. Read Reddit directly before quoting it.

Notes:

- The market splits three ways. **Map and social** apps (Fishbrain, FishAngler, onX) sell exact spots and depth contours. **Forecast** apps (Fishing Points, BassForecast, solunar calendars) sell a bite score. **Utilities** (tides, barometer, wind) do one job well. No app is great at weather-first decisions plus a private log, and Fishing Points comes closest.
- Paywalls sit on spots, contours and forecasts beyond 1–2 days. A weather-first app competes on the forecast and on trust, not on maps. Contours need licensed data (Navionics/Garmin, state agencies) that we cannot get free.
- Our own DESIGN.md already credits Windy.app's profiles and Fishing Points' 1–3 star days with major and minor times as references.

## What people complain about

### 1. Paywalls on features that used to be free
- An automated analysis of 394 recent Fishbrain reviews (August 2026) found "paywall for core features" in 52% of the negative reviews, "app used to be free" in 37% and "expensive subscription" in 28%. "Free features reduced over time" appeared in 12%. — [Glotier](https://glotier.com/apps/fishbrain-fishing-app)
- FishAngler's v5 update reportedly moved core forecast features behind VIP, and its sentiment dropped *(AI audit, small sample)*. — [Marlvel FishAngler](https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/sports/fishangler-fish-finder-app)
- Garmin raised Navionics US & Canada from $24.99 to $49.99 a year in 2023 and tied downloaded charts to an active subscription. Boaters on forums call it a lock on maps they had already paid for. — [Wavve pricing guide](https://www.wavveboating.com/?p=16552), [Fishraider thread](https://www.fishraider.com.au/topic/88443-navionics-huge-price-increase-with-replacement-app/), [SailNet "dumping Navionics"](https://www.sailnet.com/threads/dumping-navionics-garmin-looking-for-alternatives.351707/)
- One Fishing Points reviewer called the roughly $50 a year unjustified for thin inland coverage. — [Fishing Points App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishing-points-map-forecast/id1203032512)
- BassForecast keeps forecasts beyond 2 days and bait advice in PRO. Users complain about the paywall *(aggregator)*. — [Marlvel BassForecast](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/sports/com-bassforecast-bassforecastapp)

### 2. Subscription traps and billing
- Users report unexpected charges and trouble getting refunds (Fishbrain), and deceptive subscriptions and cancellation problems (Fish AI). — [Marlvel Fishbrain](https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/sports/com-fishbrain-app), [Adapty Fish AI](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/fish-ai/)
- One ANGLR user said a Pro upgrade they had bought did not show. — [JustUseApp ANGLR](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1142347232/fishing-app-anglr-logbook/problems)
- A boating app's switch to subscriptions locked users out of their own data unless they paid or exported it. This is the logbook lock-in fear in practice. — [Boating Suite reviews](https://mwm.ai/apps/boating-suite/1501874071)

### 3. Bite scores and solunar times nobody trusts
- The only modern controlled test, Quigley et al. 2023 (361 single-angler trout trips in Utah), found no significant link between catch rate and solunar values, moon phase or illumination. — [SN Applied Sciences](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42452-023-05379-8), [Wikipedia: Solunar theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solunar_theory)
- A guide's informal tally matched the solunar tables only about 22% of the time. — [Anglers Advantage](https://anglersadvantageguideservice.com/discover-the-truth-how-accurate-is-solunar-for-fishing/), [Coastal Angler "Don't be fooled by the moon"](https://coastalanglermag.com/dont-fooled-moon/)
- Reviewers report high-activity days with few fish (Fishing Points), and warn that taking BassForecast "as the Gospel" means less fishing. — [Fishing Points App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishing-points-map-forecast/id1203032512), [BassForecast App Store](https://apps.apple.com/US/app/id1088297101)
- Fishbrain's BiteTime listed species that are not in a user's lake and missed common ones. Support said the algorithm could not be changed. — [Fishbrain App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishbrain-fishing-app/id477967747)
- Evidence that barometric pressure drives bites is mostly anecdotal. Guides even disagree on whether steady or falling pressure is best. — [onX on pressure](https://www.onxmaps.com/fish/blog/how-barometric-pressure-affects-fishing-times), [AcuRite](https://www.acurite.com/blog/how-barometric-pressure-can-affect-fishing-conditions.html), [In The Spread](https://inthespread.com/blog/barometric-pressure-saltwater-fishing-science-behind-the-bite-394). **Our fishing.lua currently states pressure and moon lore as fact. See "What we must avoid".**
- "All weather apps compute from the same data", so anglers judge apps on presentation and trust, not secret accuracy. — [In-Depth Outdoors forum](https://in-depthoutdoors.com/community/forums/topic/best-weather-app) *(forum blocked fetch; seen via search summary)*

### 4. Spot burning and privacy
- Fishbrain carries a "spot burner" reputation. It blames early versions that had no privacy controls. Critics say it still exposes small spots and tags nearby locations. — [Fishbrain blog](https://fishbrain.com/blog/fishbrain/fishbrain-the-spot-burner-app), [YouTube "Why FishBrain Sucks"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3pApd0jATuA)
- Exact catch spots are the premium carrot (Fishbrain Pro, FishAngler VIP, Fishing Spots VIP), so the business model rewards exposing other people's spots. — [FishAngler](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1073941118), [Fishing Spots](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id417922502)
- Pro Angler republished a guide's reports and likeness without permission, according to his forum warning. — [Big Bend Fishing forum](https://www.bigbendfishing.net/phpBB3/viewtopic.php?p=335727)

### 5. Wrong or forced places
- Fishbrain makes users log catches at predetermined spots, often far away, and they cannot add their own. — [Fishbrain App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishbrain-fishing-app/id477967747)
- Coverage gaps: small lakes and rivers missing (Fishing Points), major Minnesota lakes missing (onX), weather only for the USA in the Gulf (FishWeather review). — [Fishing Points](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishing-points-map-forecast/id1203032512), [onX App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466307868), [FishWeather](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/fishweather/id555644333)

### 6. Inaccurate tides
- Apps snap to the nearest station, which can be far away. Two apps disagreed by 6 minutes on slack tide. A watch app was off by up to 90 minutes. Windy users in the Keys saw times about 2 hours apart, and a moderator suggested comparing stations on either side. — [Trawler Forum](https://trawlerforum.com/forums/s4/app-tides-53613.html), [Garmin forum](https://forums.garmin.com/outdoor-recreation/outdoor-recreation/f/epix-2/437025/tides-app-on-quatix-7pro), [Windy community](https://community.windy.com/topic/39247/tide-times-are-inaccurate), [Fishing Points help](https://stgfp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/26491090489490-Why-is-my-Tide-forecast-inaccurate-or-different-from-official-sources), [Charleston Fishing forum](https://forum.charlestonfishing.com/t/tide-chart-off/99577)

### 7. Logbooks: lock-in, no export, too much clutter
- BassResource members treat "no map, no data export" as a dealbreaker for a log app *(thread blocked fetch; seen via search summary)*. — [BassResource](https://www.bassresource.com/bass-fishing-forums/topic/248532-what-is-the-best-fish-catch-log-app/)
- Several log apps gate CSV export behind Pro (FishLogger, Fishing Journal) or sell it separately (Fishing Friend). Newer apps advertise "no subscription, one-tap export" as a selling point. — [FishLogger](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6757573416), [Fishing Friend](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishing-friend/id508487103), [Anglers' Log](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id959989008)

### 8. Bloat, bugs and redesigns
- FishAngler's v5 brought a complex UI redesign. Fishbrain users report bugs and lower value after updates while paying the same price. — [Marlvel FishAngler](https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/sports/fishangler-fish-finder-app), [Fishbrain App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishbrain-fishing-app/id477967747), [UnitQ FishAngler](https://www.unitQ.com/unitq-scorecards/fishangler/)

### 9. Water temperature is missing or vague
- Lake surface temperature exists only from satellites (LakeMonster, Omnia, Windy), works best as a trend, and misses small lakes and cloudy days. Some reviews complain that apps have no surface temperature at all. — [Omnia](https://www.omniafishing.com/a/is-omnias-water-temperature-layer-accurate), [Windy lake temperature](https://media.windy.app/lake_temperature), [MSU CoastWatch](https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/use_coastwatch_for_fantastic_fishing_this_spring_okeefe16)

## Persona

### Primary: Dave, the weekend lake angler

- **Who:** Dave Becker, 46, maintenance supervisor in central Wisconsin. He has fished for 30 years and owns a 16 ft aluminium boat with a small outboard. He fishes four or five lakes within an hour, mostly for walleye, bass and pike, and ice fishes in winter.
- **Context:** He gets one or two half-days a week. He decides the evening before or at 5 a.m. in the kitchen. He goes alone or with his teenage son, so safety matters.
- **Goals:**
  - Pick the best day this week and the best 2–3 hours of it.
  - Know whether wind will make his bank or his boat unsafe.
  - See whether a front is coming, and remember what worked last time in similar conditions.
- **Frustrations:**
  - Fishbrain put the useful bits behind a $80-a-year paywall, and he hates its feed and the people who post his spots.
  - He doesn't believe the "4 fish" moon ratings.
  - Wind comes in km/h or m/s when he thinks in mph.
  - His notes are scattered across the Notes app and photos.
- **Devices:** iPhone 13 in a waterproof case, often with wet or gloved hands. An Apple Watch is a maybe. Signal on the lake is patchy.
- **Use:** About 30 seconds at a glance the night before (week view, best window). A look at the dock (wind, gusts, radar-ish rain, pressure). A quick catch log with one hand on the water. A look back at his log in winter.

### Secondary: Ana, the shore angler on a tidal coast

- **Who:** Ana Ribeiro, 33, nurse in Portugal on shift work. She fishes from rocks and beaches for sea bass and bream and travels to new coasts on holiday.
- **Goals:** Know the tide stage and the swell on her rocks and the sea temperature, and get a "you can go after your shift" answer.
- **Frustrations:** Tide apps that are US-only or pick a far station. Swell that makes her rocks dangerous. Apps that are not in her units or language.
- **Use:** She checks after a shift for the next 48 hours, wants an alert when a calm low tide falls at dawn on her day off, and finds new places by searching while travelling.

## Feature gaps

Ordered by impact on a successful App Store launch. Sizes: S ≈ days, M ≈ 1–2 weeks, L ≈ several weeks or engine work.

1. **My places: location, search and saved spots**
   - **What:** Device location, place search, saved and pinned private places, each with a kind (lake, river, coast) and a facing.
   - **Why:** Stories 12, 56–63 and complaint 5. A curated list of six fishing places cannot hold a user.
   - **Data:** [Open-Meteo Geocoding API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api) (free). Location needs CoreLocation; check whether ORCA exposes it to Lua, it probably does not. Persistence needs local storage.
   - **Size:** L.
   - **Files:** `places.lua`, `provider.lua`, `App.lua`, `Pages/Hub.xml`, `Data/Hub.xml`, `Templates/PlaceRow.xml`, plus an engine/iOS location bridge.

2. **Units and locale**
   - **What:** mph, knots, °F, inHg and ft, chosen by region with a setting.
   - **Why:** Stories 17, 21 and 99. US anglers, the largest market, think in mph and inHg. m/s is unusual outside science and sailing.
   - **Data:** None. Convert at display time. Open-Meteo also accepts `wind_speed_unit`, `temperature_unit` and `precipitation_unit`, but the rules are written in m/s, so keep the fetch metric.
   - **Size:** M.
   - **Files:** `score.lua` (`fmt`), `report.lua`, `activities/fishing.lua` (`note`, `metrics`, chart titles), `Styles`.

3. **Tides for coastal places**
   - **What:** High and low times, a tide curve on "today", the tide stage in the score ("moving water", "slack"), and the station name and distance.
   - **Why:** Stories 31–36 and 40, and complaint 6. Tides are the first thing saltwater anglers ask for, and README lists them as not built.
   - **Data:**
     - **US:** [NOAA CO-OPS API](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/), free public data. It offers harmonic predictions and high/low times for subordinate stations.
     - **Worldwide fallback:** Open-Meteo Marine `sea_level_height_msl`, about 8 km, including tides. Open-Meteo says it is not for coastal navigation and may be unreliable inland. Label it "approximate" and derive high and low from its turning points.
     - **Better worldwide:** a paid service (WorldTides etc.) *(not evaluated)*.
   - **Size:** L.
   - **Files:** `forecast.lua` (a new tide request and parse), `activities/fishing.lua` (rules, a third chart or replace wind), `report.lua`, `Data/Activity.xml`, `places.lua` (station id for curated places), `tests/test_weather.lua`.

4. **Water temperature**
   - **What:** Sea temperature on coasts. A modelled water-surface estimate for big lakes, labelled as an estimate. A field for the user's own reading. Use it in the score when known.
   - **Why:** Stories 37, 41–43, 48 and complaint 9.
   - **Data:**
     - Marine `sea_surface_temperature`, which `forecast.lua` already parses but the fishing edition never fetches: fishing has `needsMarine = false`, and in the edition no other activity asks for the sea. Set it to `"optional"` for coast places.
     - For lakes, Open-Meteo documents `soil_temperature_0cm` as the "water surface temperature on water" grid cells. It is already fetched, but only means water when the cell is water, so large lakes only *(uncertain; verify per lake)*.
   - **Size:** S (sea), M (lake estimate and manual entry).
   - **Files:** `activities/fishing.lua`, `activities.lua`, `forecast.lua`, `report.lua`.

5. **Private catch log tied to conditions**
   - **What:** A two-tap log that works offline and snapshots the whole hour (pressure and trend, wind, cloud, moon, tide, water temperature). Optional species, size, lure and photo. Skunk trips. Free CSV/JSON export and an iCloud backup.
   - **Why:** Stories 68–76, complaints 2, 4 and 7. This is the retention engine and the honest replacement for bite scores.
   - **Data:**
     - Our own forecast for the current hour.
     - For catches entered later, the forecast API `past_days` (up to 92) or the [Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api).
     - Local persistent storage and file export, possibly missing in ORCA. Check the engine.
   - **Size:** L.
   - **Files:** a new `logbook.lua`, `Pages/Log.xml`, `Templates/CatchRow.xml`, `Data/Log.xml`, `App.lua` routes, `Data/Routes.xml`, `Styles`.

6. **Pressure history and fronts**
   - **What:** The past 48 hours plus the next 24 on the pressure chart, a sentence that names the front and its hour ("front passes ~14:00", "day 2 after a cold front"), and a trend over 6 hours as well as 3.
   - **Why:** Stories 15, 16 and 18. Pressure is the one thing anglers trust most, and currently only the next 24 hours show.
   - **Data:** Open-Meteo `past_days=2`, already free. Fronts can be inferred from the pressure minimum, a wind shift and a temperature drop.
   - **Size:** S–M.
   - **Files:** `forecast.lua` (`past_days`, so `now` indexing changes), `activities/fishing.lua` (`charts`, rules), `report.lua`.

7. **Honest scoring and personal patterns**

     - Show "days like today" from the log.

   - **Files:** `activities/fishing.lua`, `score.lua`, the new log module, `Templates/ReasonRow.xml`.

## What we must avoid

- **Presenting lore as science.** `activities/fishing.lua` states pressure, moon and light rules as facts ("Near the full moon: a strong feeding day"). Keep the rules, but phrase them as anglers' experience, let users switch the moon off, and let the personal log overrule them. The best study found no solunar effect.
- **Pulling features behind a paywall later.** This is the single biggest complaint against Fishbrain and FishAngler. Decide the free and paid line before launch and never move free features up. The forecast and the basic log stay free.
- **Ads, dark-pattern trials and hard-to-cancel subscriptions.** Prefer a one-time unlock or a low, clear yearly price for extras: widgets, alerts, iCloud sync, species profiles.
- **A social feed or a public spot map.** That brings spot burning, moderation and clutter. Any sharing should be an explicit export of one catch, with the location rounded or removed.
- **Locking the user's log.** Export must be free and complete, and stay working even if they stop paying.
- **Silent tide guesses.** Always show the station or source and its distance, and label Open-Meteo sea-level tides as approximate. Never present them as navigation data.
- **Claiming lake water temperature we don't have.** Label model or satellite estimates and let users enter real readings.
- **Calling a dangerous hour "good".** Lightning, gusts, swell on rocks and cold water must cap the score, and capped reasons come first.
- **Forcing places on users** (Fishbrain's predetermined spots). Users can forecast any pin they choose.
- **Unit and timezone mistakes.** m/s for US users, or tide times in the device's timezone instead of the place's, destroy trust quickly.
- **Bloat.** Keep the Windows Phone directness: the verdict, the best window and the reasons in the first screen. Maps, contours and sonar are other apps' games; we cannot license that data for free.
- **Shipping on the free Open-Meteo tier commercially.**
