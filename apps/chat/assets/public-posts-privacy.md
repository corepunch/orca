## Public posts in Chat

The Posts area uses the shared Octopus service. Text, photos, quotes, links,
tags, replies, likes, reposts and your public profile bio appear in the app
and on the Octopus website. These can be read without signing in. Following
relationships are visible to signed-in users.

Sharing a public post into a conversation adds its title and public link to
a private message draft. You review and send that message explicitly. Sharing
does not publish the conversation, its messages or its private photos.
