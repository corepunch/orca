# Sign-in brand marks

Single-colour masks for `VectorView`, which tints each SVG with its
Foreground. Not Lucide icons: providers require their own marks.

- `apple.svg`: the Apple logo for "Continue with Apple", shown in the button's
  text colour per Apple's Sign in with Apple guidelines.
- `google-{blue,green,yellow,red}.svg`: the four segments of Google's "G"
  (48×48 viewBox, from Google Identity Services' button). Stacked and tinted
  #4285F4, #34A853, #FBBC05 and #EA4335, they form the full-colour logo Google's
  branding guidelines require on a white button.
