# Octopus Community Rules

Last updated: November 14, 2026

These rules apply to posts, photos, quotes, links, comments, tags and public
profiles in Octopus and on the Octopus website. By creating an account you
agree to follow them. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or
abusive behaviour.

## Keep the timeline safe

- Do not threaten, harass, bully or target people with hateful abuse.
- Do not promote violence, exploitation or illegal activity, or encourage
  someone to harm themselves or another person.
- Do not share sexual content involving children, non-consensual intimate
  images, or content that exploits or endangers a child.
- Do not post pornography or sexually explicit content.
- Do not expose another person's private information or intimate content
  without their permission.
- Do not impersonate others, defraud people, post phishing links or malware,
  or use the service for spam and repeated unwanted contact.
- Share only content you have the right to share. Do not infringe other
  people's copyright or other rights.
- Do not evade blocks, suspensions or content restrictions, or misuse
  reporting tools to harass someone.

## Report a concern privately

Open a post's menu and choose **report post**, choose **report** under a
comment, or open a person's profile menu and choose **report**. Pick a reason
to send a private report; you can add relevant details first. A report keeps
a copy of the reported text and references to its photos as review context.
Report only the content or behaviour involved; do not use reports to
retaliate against someone.

For abuse reports or questions about these rules, email
[igor.chernakov@gmail.com](mailto:igor.chernakov@gmail.com). Include the
account or post involved and enough context to identify the concern. Do not
include passwords.

## Blocking

Choose **block** from a post's menu or a person's profile menu. Their posts,
reposts, comments and activity leave your timelines at once, and they leave
search and suggestions. Blocks are shared with Orca Messenger, where they
also prevent direct contact. Manage them under **me › blocked people**.

## Content and account action

Reports are reviewed by people, normally within 24 hours. We remove content
that violates these rules and may restrict or remove the accounts that
posted it. Serious or repeated violations result in loss of access to the
service.

If you believe content or account action was taken in error, contact the
email above and explain the issue.

Read the [privacy policy](https://corepunch.github.io/orca/chat/privacy/) for
information about service data and how to delete your account.
