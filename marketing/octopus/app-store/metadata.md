**Description:**
Octopus is a small, calm place to share posts and follow people, with a bold sideways-swiping interface.

SWIPE SIDEWAYS
Home, connect and discover sit side by side. Swipe or tap a header to move between your timeline, what happened to your posts, and new people to follow.

YOUR TIMELINE, IN ORDER
Home shows your posts, the people you follow and what they reposted, newest first. No ranking, no guessing.

FOUR WAYS TO POST
Write a text post with a title, share up to nine photos, pull out a quote, or recommend a link, each with tags.

CONVERSATIONS
Like, repost and reply. Every post opens into its threaded replies.

READ FIRST, SIGN IN LATER
Browse discover, posts, profiles and search without an account. Sign in when you want to post, like or follow.

Octopus shares its posts and people with the Octopus website. Built with ORCA, an open-source application framework.
