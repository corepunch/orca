# Orca Messenger Community Rules

Last updated: November 8, 2026

These rules apply to messages, photos, public profiles, conversation names,
groups and channels in Orca Messenger.

## Keep conversations safe

- Do not threaten, harass, bully or target people with hateful abuse.
- Do not promote violence, exploitation or illegal activity, or encourage
  someone to harm themselves or another person.
- Do not share sexual content involving children, non-consensual intimate
  images, or content that exploits or endangers a child.
- Do not post pornography or sexually explicit content.
- Do not expose another person's private information or intimate content
  without their permission.
- Do not impersonate others, defraud people, send phishing links or malware,
  or use the service for spam and repeated unwanted contact.
- Share only content you have the right to share. Do not infringe other
  people's copyright or other rights.
- Do not evade blocks, suspensions or content restrictions, interfere with
  other users' access, or misuse reporting tools to harass someone.

## Report a concern privately

Choose **report or block** from an incoming message's actions or another
member's row in conversation info. On that page, choose the reason to submit
a private report; you can add relevant details first. A message report
preserves the reported text and attachment references as review context.
Report only the content or behavior involved; do not use reports to retaliate
against someone.

For abuse reports or questions about these rules, email
[igor.chernakov@gmail.com](mailto:igor.chernakov@gmail.com). Include the account
or conversation involved and enough relevant context to identify the concern.
Do not include passwords or sign-in codes. Share only the private information
needed for the report.

The public GitHub issue tracker is for general product support. Do not post
private conversations, account information or abuse reports there.

## Blocking and content checks

Blocking an account prevents direct contact and some discovery, channel and
invitation paths between you. Existing direct-chat history remains readable,
and blocking does not hide messages in groups you already share. You can
leave a shared group if you no longer want to participate.

The service rejects some objectionable text using server-side word and phrase
checks on messages and public names. The checks have limited coverage.
Photos and other reported content can be reviewed by people; there is no
automated image classifier assessing photo content.

## Content and account action

We may review reported content and relevant service records, remove content
that violates these rules, restrict access, or suspend abusive accounts.
Serious or repeated violations may result in loss of access to the service.
Group and channel owners and admins can also remove messages according to
their roles. Those conversation roles do not replace service-level review.

If you believe content or account action was taken in error, contact the
private email above and explain the issue. Reports and appeals are reviewed
by people.

Read the [privacy policy](https://corepunch.github.io/orca/chat/privacy/) for
information about service data, public profile photos and privacy requests.
